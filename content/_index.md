### An easy to use and powerful harmony  and chord exploration device 🎵

**ChoCo** (short for Chord Composer) is a compact USB‑MIDI controller inspired by synthesizer [HiChord by Pocket Audio](https://hichord.shop/).

ChoCo is built on Raspberry Pi Pico (RP2040) that lets you trigger in-scale/key chords from a 7 keys keypad and "shape" them with the movement of a joystick (7ths, 9ths, sus, dim, etc.).
It features also an OLED screen to display useful information like the chord you're playing and a "combo" history of the last triggered chords, useful to re-play a progression you liked.
