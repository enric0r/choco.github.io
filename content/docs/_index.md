ChoCo is an RP2040 USB MIDI controller. Start with the path that matches what you want to do:

1. [Get started]({{< relref "docs/getting_started" >}}) to connect a board and play your first chord.
2. [Controls]({{< relref "docs/controls" >}}) for the complete keypad and joystick reference.
3. [Hardware]({{< relref "docs/building" >}}) for the firmware pin map and KiCad source files.
4. [Firmware and troubleshooting]({{< relref "docs/flashing_debugging" >}}) to build, flash, and diagnose the device.
5. [Configuration and development]({{< relref "docs/development" >}}) for settings, architecture, and tests.

These pages follow the current source in [enric0r/ChoCo](https://github.com/enric0r/ChoCo). Hardware behavior, MIDI delivery, and display appearance should also be checked on a physical board.
